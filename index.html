Help

Incipit gives you something to write every day: an opening line or a famous line from world literature, or an original prompt, and short warm-ups. Your writing stays on your device.

Free trial and subscription

How does the free trial work?

The annual and monthly plans begin with a free trial. Two days before it ends, Incipit reminds you with a notification, if you’ve allowed them. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end and you won’t be charged.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

In Incipit, open the Settings tab and tap Manage subscription. You can also do it in the Settings app on your device: tap your name, then Subscriptions.

I have a new device, or I reinstalled Incipit

Open Incipit, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy.

Can I use Incipit on iPad and Mac?

Yes: on iPhone and iPad, and on Macs with Apple silicon, with the same purchase. Your writing isn’t synced between devices yet: each device keeps its own.

Your writing

Where is my writing saved?

Only on your device. To keep a copy, go to Settings and tap Export all your writing.

What happens if I delete the app?

Your writing is deleted with it. Export it first.

Prompts and quotations

Where do the quotations come from?

From works whose authors died more than a hundred years ago, or from anonymous works, checked against the original text. The translations were made for Incipit. Read the book opens the complete work on Wikisource, and under each quotation Show the original lets you read it in its own language and script.

Which languages does Incipit speak?

English, Italian, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese. Incipit follows the language you choose for it in the Settings app.

Voice

Dictation doesn’t start

Check that Incipit can use the microphone and speech recognition: Settings app › Incipit. Whether recognition can happen on the device itself depends on the language and on your device.
